I found Wild Haus the way I find most things in this city now: a coworker mentioned it in passing and I became mildly obsessed within the week. It's a wood-fired barrel sauna that shows up at a handful of spots around the water, and the whole thing runs on a booking app like it's a dinner reservation, which should tell you how popular it's gotten.

The format is simple and a little brutal. Twenty minutes in the heat until you can't stand it anymore, then straight into the cold plunge (a literal barrel of ice water, no easing in allowed), then back to the heat. Repeat two or three times. Somewhere around round two your brain just stops narrating and you're only thinking about your own breathing, which is apparently the whole point and also apparently why I keep going back even though the plunge never, ever stops being awful for the first ten seconds.

## Before you go

- **Book at least a few days out.** Evening slots, especially weekends, fill up fast.
- **Bring a towel and water shoes.** The dock gets slick, and you'll thank yourself.
- **Go at sunset if you can.** The heat is the same at any hour, but the view is not.
- **Don't eat a big meal right before.** Learned this one the hard way.

It's not cheap, and it's not a spontaneous plan, but it's the one splurge I don't talk myself out of anymore. I leave every single time feeling like I got a full reset on a week that hadn't earned one. If cold plunges aren't your thing, [floating in total silence](/journal/float-seattle) is the gentler version of the same reset.
