Four days is a strange amount of time for a city like Amsterdam. Too short to see everything, long enough that you start to feel like you live there, which is honestly the better outcome anyway.

I spent day one intentionally jet-lagged and wandering with no plan, which I now think is the correct way to arrive anywhere. Day two was a rented bike and a very sore, very worth it set of legs. Day three was a museum I won't spoil, and a canal-side dinner that ran three hours longer than planned. Day four I just repeated whatever had felt best so far, which is my actual rule for the last day of any trip.
